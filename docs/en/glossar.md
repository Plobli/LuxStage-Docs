# Glossary

Theatre and lighting terms as used in LuxStage.

**Lighting rig** — A tower or rig with several numbered spots (slots) for fixtures. Managed in the [Setup](./webapp/setup-gestelle) area.

**DMX** — Digital Multiplex, the standard control protocol for stage lighting. Every fixture is assigned a DMX address, through which the lighting console addresses it.

**Dimmer address vs. channel number** — The **channel number** is the name under which a fixture is addressed in the lighting console (freely chosen). The **dimmer address** is the actual DMX address of the dimmer unit. Both appear side by side in the channel table, separated by "/" (e.g. "1/001").

**Fly system** — The technical rigging above the stage (flying bars, backdrops, lighting). In LuxStage, the internal name for the bars sub-tab within Setup.

**Gel** — Colour filter placed in front of a fixture, named after a manufacturer code (e.g. LEE "L202" or Rosco "R02"). In LuxStage, selectable via dropdown, free text, or "No Color".

**Hanging plot** — The complete set of hung lighting positions for a show. In LuxStage, the basis for the automatically generated text in the Info tab.

**Gassenturm** — A side lighting tower, usually placed in the stage wings. In LuxStage, the internal name of the "Setup" tab.

**Portal bridge** — A fixed lighting bridge at the stage portal, usually with permanently mounted fixture positions.

**Position** — The installation or hanging point of a fixture (e.g. "FOH BAR LEFT", "OVERHEAD BAR 1"). Channels are grouped by position in the channel table.

**Slot** — A single spot on a lighting rig that a channel can be assigned to.

**Venue template** — A reusable template with a fixed channel structure for a stage or venue, which can be applied to any new show.

**Bar** — A horizontal, usually height-adjustable pipe for hanging fixtures, with freely positionable spots along its length (unlike a lighting rig with fixed slots).
