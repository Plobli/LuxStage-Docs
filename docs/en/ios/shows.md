# Shows (iOS)

The **Shows** tab is the home screen of the app. It lists all existing shows as tiles, grouped by venue.

## Shows overview

<img src="./img/shows-uebersicht.png" alt="Shows overview" class="ios-screenshot">

Each tile contains the **show name** and the **date** of the production. Shows without an assigned venue appear below a separator line; shows with a venue are grouped under the respective venue name as a heading (e.g. "Venue 1").

Tapping a tile opens the show directly in the **Setup** tab.

## Create a new show

1. Tap the **+** button in the top right
2. Enter show name, date and (optionally) venue in the dialog
3. Confirm — the show appears immediately in the list

::: tip
If a venue is selected, the new show automatically inherits the channel template and floor plan of the chosen venue.
:::
