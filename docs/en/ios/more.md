# Photos & Floor Plan (iOS)

Behind the **More** tab (three dots) are the visual documentation views of the show: **Photos** and **Floor Plan**.

## Photos

The Photos view shows all images uploaded to the show. Photos are managed in the web app and linked to channel numbers — in the iOS app they are then available to view.

### Channel filter

<img src="/img/ios/more-fotos-galerie.png" alt="Photo gallery" class="ios-screenshot">

At the top of the view there are filter buttons:

- **All channels** — shows all photos in the show
- **Channel x, y, z** — shows only photos assigned to specific channel numbers in the web app (e.g. "Channel 651, 652, 653, 654")

### Full screen

Tapping a photo opens the full-screen view. The assigned **channel numbers** are shown at the bottom (e.g. `# 651, 652, 653, 654`). Tap **Done** to return to the gallery.

<img src="/img/ios/more-foto-vollbild.png" alt="Photo full screen" class="ios-screenshot">

### Add photos

The **+** button in the top right allows photos to be added directly in the iOS app.

::: tip Focus reference
During focusing, the reference photos assigned to the current channel are quickly accessible via **More → Photos** — ideal for comparing the focus position without switching between apps.
:::

## Floor Plan

The floor plan shows the stored stage plan for the show. It is uploaded in the web app and is available read-only in the iOS app.
